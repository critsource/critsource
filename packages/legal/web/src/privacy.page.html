    • In short

    • No accounts, no analytics, and no ads. The only cookie is a security cookie from Cloudflare.
    • Our server logs each request, including your IP address, to keep the site running and safe. It deletes the logs after 14 days.
    • Your theme choice stays in your browser and never reaches us.
    • Questions or requests about your data go to {{ supportEmail }}.

    Who is responsible

    Critsource, an open-source project run by volunteers and led by Oscar Wellner in the Netherlands, is responsible for the personal data this site processes.

    Email {{ supportEmail }} with any question about your data. We answer within one month, as the GDPR requires.

    What we process

    We process as little as we can. This table lists all of it.

    Data we process
    Data Why Legal basis Kept for
    Server logs Your IP address, the time, the page you asked for, the page you came from, and your browser. We use them to deliver pages, find errors, and stop abuse. Legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f) 14 days
    Bot check Cloudflare runs a script and sets the cf_clearance cookie to tell people from bots and protect the site from abuse. Legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f) 6 months, in your browser
    Theme choice Dark or Light, if you pick one, so the next visit opens in that theme. It stays in your browser’s local storage and is never sent to us. None needed: it stays on your device Until you pick System or clear your site data
    Emails to us Your address and what you write, so we can answer you. Legitimate interest, GDPR art. 6(1)(f) 1 year after your question is resolved

    What we don’t do

    • Set cookies that track you or need your consent.
    • Run analytics, tracking pixels, or ads.
    • Load fonts from other companies; we host them ourselves. The only outside script is Cloudflare’s bot check.
    • Sell your data or share it for marketing.

    Who else sees it

    We run the server ourselves, in the Netherlands, and it keeps its logs there. Cloudflare delivers every page to you, so it sees your IP address and each request, and it runs the bot check. It does so on our behalf, under its data processing agreement, and may transfer the data to the US under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

    Email to {{ supportEmail }} goes through Cloudflare to a Gmail mailbox, so Google stores it too, also outside the EU, under its own privacy policy.

    Links to GitHub take you to a site that processes your data itself. Read GitHub’s privacy statement to see how.

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or object to its use. Email {{ supportEmail }}. Logs hold only an IP address, so we may ask for your address and the time of your visit to find your entries.

    If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, the Dutch data protection authority.
