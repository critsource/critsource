    Our goal

    Critsource should work for every player at the table, whether you use a keyboard, a screen reader, a magnifier, or voice control. We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 at level AA, in both the dark and the light theme.

    What we’ve done

    • Every page works with a keyboard alone, starting with a link that skips to the main content.
    • Text and controls meet the AA contrast ratios in both themes.
    • Pages use headings, landmarks, and labels that screen readers announce.
    • Text follows your browser’s font size, and pages work at 200% zoom without losing content.
    • Nothing moves on its own: previews never rotate, and animations follow your reduced-motion setting.
    • The theme follows your device, and you can pick Dark or Light yourself.

    Known issues

    We know of no barriers right now.

    Every open accessibility issue is on GitHub, under the accessibility label.

    How we test

    Lint rules catch common mistakes in our templates before they ship. We test each page with a keyboard, with NVDA in Firefox and VoiceOver in Safari, at 200% zoom, and in both themes. No outside audit has been done yet.

    Report a barrier

    If something on the site doesn’t work for you, tell us. Describe the page, what you tried to do, and the browser and assistive technology you use. We reply within 10 working days.

    Email support Open an issue on GitHub
    An issue on GitHub is public. Email us if you’d rather not share the details.
